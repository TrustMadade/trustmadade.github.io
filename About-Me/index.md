# 👋 Hello, I'm Trust

I'm a Marketing Analyst originally from **Zimre Park**, a modern suburb in **Ruwa, Zimbabwe**, just outside **Harare**. In September 2024, I relocated to **San Francisco** to pursue a **Master’s in Marketing and Analytics**, driven by my passion for data-powered decision-making and human-centered marketing strategy.

With a solid foundation in **statistical analysis** and **data visualization**, I specialize in translating complex datasets into actionable insights that help brands optimize performance, improve engagement, and increase ROI.

---

## 🧰 Technical Toolkit

I’m proficient in a range of tools and techniques that allow me to bring structure, creativity, and insight to every project:

- **Languages & Data Tools**: SQL, NoSQL, Python (Pandas, Matplotlib), SPSS, Excel  
- **Visualization & BI**: Tableau, Power BI  
- **Marketing & Analytics Platforms**: Google Analytics, A/B testing, campaign performance tracking  

---

## 💡 What Drives Me

To me, marketing is where **art meets science** — a dynamic blend of creativity and analytical rigor. I thrive on uncovering patterns in consumer behavior, building strategies that respond to emerging trends, and crafting stories that connect data with decisions.

When I’m not analyzing campaigns or building dashboards, you’ll find me exploring new cities, learning from different cultures, and tasting the world through food.

---

> I believe the best strategies are built on **insight, empathy, and evidence** — and I’m always looking to collaborate on projects that create measurable impact.
