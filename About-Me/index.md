## Hello, I’m Trust!  

I hail from Zimre Park, a modern suburb of Ruwa, nestled right on the outskirts of Harare, Zimbabwe. In September 2024, I made the leap to San Francisco to pursue my Master’s in Marketing and Analytics, delving deeper into the realm of data-driven strategies and consumer insights. With a background in statistical analysis and data visualization, I excel at transforming raw data into engaging narratives that enhance marketing performance. 

My skill set includes: **SQL, NoSQL, Python, SPSS, Excel, Tableau, Power BI, and Google Analytics**, which enables me to help brands refine their strategies and maximize their return on investment (ROI).  I firmly believe that marketing blends art with science, and that gaining insights into consumer behavior through analytics leads to smarter, more effective decision-making. Whether it’s anticipating trends or boosting engagement, I take pride in crafting innovative strategies to drive business success.  
When I’m not immersed in marketing analytics, I love exploring new cities, soaking up different cultures, and savoring diverse global cuisines.
