# Fix Chocolate Knafeh U.S. Launch – Instagram Campaign

## Project Overview

As part of MIMA Team 6, we partnered with the Dubai-based dessert brand Fix Dessert Chocolatier to design an Instagram launch campaign for their viral product — the Pistachio Knafeh Chocolate Bar — targeting the U.S. market. Our goal was to build awareness, drive online traffic, and convert views into product sales through Meta’s ad ecosystem.

---

## My Role and Contributions

I contributed to the Instagram media strategy, audience segmentation, and paid campaign design, focusing on performance forecasting, ad copy, and influencer targeting.

---

## Campaign Highlights

- Target Markets: New York and California  
- Campaign 1 – Awareness: Reached 28,000+ impressions  
- Ad Formats: Reels, Stories, and Feed Carousel  
- Budget Strategy: $120 total flight, audience-tested
