# 📱 Facebook Competitor Analysis – Cosmetics Industry

## 🎯 Project Overview

In this solo project, I acted as a data intern for a growing cosmetics company seeking business intelligence on Facebook strategies used by a major competitor during their key growth years (2012–2017). I analyzed social media data using Power BI and developed an **interactive dashboard** to identify what content types, posting strategies, and messaging drove the most engagement.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Methods

- 🟧 **Power BI** (interactive dashboard creation)
- 📊 DAX for calculated metrics
- 📄 Text analysis and keyword extraction
- 📆 Time-series and categorical filtering

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

- 📷 **Post Type Dominance**: Photos were overwhelmingly used (1,967 posts) compared to videos, links, or statuses.
- ❤️ **Highest Likes**: Photo posts had the highest average likes per post — indicating visual content performed best.
- ⏰ **Comment Engagement**: Posts receiving the most comments were typically published late at night and early mornings.
- 💬 **Top Keywords**: Words like “love,” “perfect,” and “beautiful” were commonly used, supporting a brand voice focused on positivity and admiration.
- 🔥 **Most Liked Posts**: Messages that asked users to engage (“click like if…”, “who’s with us…”) drove significantly more likes.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- ✅ **Likes & Engagement by Post Type**
- ✅ **Comment Frequency by Hour of Day**
- ✅ **Keyword Cloud by Message Text**
- ✅ **Top-Performing Messages (Likes)**
- ✅ Dynamic filters by post type and year
