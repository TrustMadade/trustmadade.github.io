# VRBO Global Expansion – Heritage Strategy

## Project Overview

As part of Team 5 MIMA, our challenge was to propose a strategy that enables VRBO to differentiate itself in international markets by attracting a new type of supply partner — vacation rental owners near UNESCO heritage and cultural sites.
Our pitch focused on the intersection of cultural preservation, authentic travel experiences, and responsible tourism.

---

## My Role & Contributions

I co-developed the expansion strategy and helped shape the owner incentive model, audience targeting, and revenue projections based on VRBO’s existing fee structure and emerging market opportunities outside the U.S.

---

## Strategy Highlights

- Market Opportunity: Global heritage tourism market projected to reach $604.4B
- Owner Targeting: Focus on historical homeowners, cultural preservationists, and family-run estates
- Property Types: Château stays (France), temple lodgings (Asia), monasteries (Europe)
- Monetization Model: VRBO fee (15%) + voluntary cultural contribution (20%) to fund site preservation
- Value Proposition: Differentiation via social responsibility, niche inventory, and emotional appeal to heritage
