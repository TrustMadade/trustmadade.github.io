# 📊 Hult Superstore – Sales & Marketing Dashboard

## 🎯 Project Overview

This individual Tableau project explores sales and profitability trends in the **Hult Superstore dataset**, with the goal of informing **targeted advertising and product strategy** across the U.S. Using a set of interactive dashboards, I visualized category-level performance, geographic sales distribution, and profitability margins — empowering smarter marketing allocation decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Methods

- 🧰 **Tableau** (dashboard creation, forecasting, filtering)
- 📊 **Data visualization** best practices
- 📍 **Sub-category filtering**, **regional heatmaps**, and **profit-performance overlays**

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- **Sales by Sub-Category**: Bar chart comparing total sales across all sub-categories. Filters dynamically update all other views.
- **Regional Heat Map**: U.S. map showing sales distribution, with darker shades representing higher volume.
- **Profit Chart**: Visualizes profitability vs. sales to identify high-margin vs. high-volume products.
- **Accessibility**: All charts are paired with captions and alt-text to support inclusive analysis.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- 💡 **Top Sellers**: Phones ($330K) and Chairs ($328K) lead in total revenue.
- 💰 **Most Profitable**: Copiers (37% margin) and Paper (43% margin) outperform others in profit %, despite lower total sales.
- 🗺️ **Top States**: California ($457K), New York ($310K), Texas, and Washington are key sales regions.
- ⚠️ **Profit Gaps**: Tables and Bookcases had $321K+ in revenue but resulted in net losses.

---

## 📑 Strategy Recommendations

- ✅ **Reallocate ads** toward Copiers and Paper for better ROI
- 🧭 **Geographic focus** on high-potential markets like CA, NY, TX, and WA
- 🎯 **Niche targeting** in underperforming but promising states (e.g., North Dakota, Maine)
- 🔁 **Ongoing dashboard monitoring** to adjust strategies in real-time
